WHO WE ARE

A Global Business & Technology Partner

Aman Global International Services (AGIS) is a Nepal-based international business and technology services company focused on helping organizations build, improve and grow their digital businesses.

Our vision is to connect professional services, modern technology, artificial intelligence, accounting, analytics and digital solutions into one global platform.

We aim to serve startups, small businesses, educational organizations, professional firms and growing companies across different markets.
